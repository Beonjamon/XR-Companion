XR Companion privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

XR Companion ("the app") is a floating companion for Android XR glasses and headsets, made by chaoticProgress. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Summary

The app does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal information. It has no account, no analytics and no advertising of its own.

Hand and head tracking

The app asks for the hand tracking permission and uses your head pose so the companion can follow you, react to your gestures and stay in view. This data is processed on your device, in real time, and is never sent anywhere or kept after use.

Scene understanding

The app asks for the scene understanding permission to read QR codes you choose to show it (for example a link to a video). Camera images are not saved or sent by the app; only the decoded link is used, on your device.

Settings

Your choices (size, colours, timer and metronome settings and similar) are saved in the app's private storage on your device. They are not backed up or sent anywhere, and uninstalling the app removes them.

YouTube in the pane

The pane can show YouTube's mobile website (m.youtube.com) inside the app. When you use it, your device connects directly to YouTube, which receives what any web browser would send, and if you sign in, your Google account. YouTube's use of that data is covered by Google's Privacy Policy and YouTube's Terms of Service. The app does not read, store or send your YouTube activity or your sign-in details. YouTube's cookies are kept in the app's private storage on your device so you stay signed in; clearing the app's data signs you out.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
